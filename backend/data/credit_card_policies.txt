Frequently Asked Questions: Credit Card Policies

What credit card options are available?
We offer cashback cards, travel rewards cards, low-interest cards, and secured credit cards for customers building or rebuilding credit. Each card has a different annual fee structure and rewards program.

How is my credit limit determined?
Your credit limit is based on your income, existing debt obligations, and credit history at the time of application. Limits are reviewed periodically and may increase automatically based on responsible usage over time.

What happens if I miss a payment?
A missed payment may result in a late fee and can affect your interest rate through a penalty APR. Payments more than 30 days late are typically reported to credit bureaus, which can lower your credit score.

How do I report credit card fraud?
If you notice unauthorized transactions, contact us immediately through the mobile app, website, or 24-hour fraud hotline. We will freeze the affected card, issue a replacement, and investigate the charges. You are not liable for confirmed fraudulent transactions reported promptly.

What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?
Lock your card instantly through the mobile app to prevent new transactions, then report it as lost or stolen through the app or by calling customer service. A replacement card is typically issued within five to seven business days, with expedited shipping available.

Are there protections against unauthorized use?
Yes, all cardholders are covered by zero-liability protection for unauthorized transactions, provided the loss is reported promptly. We also offer real-time transaction alerts and the ability to temporarily lock your card if you suspect suspicious activity.
