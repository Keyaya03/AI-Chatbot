Frequently Asked Questions: Loan Types

What types of loans do you offer?
We offer personal loans, auto loans, student loans, mortgages, and home equity lines of credit. Personal loans can be used for almost any purpose, while auto and student loans are tied to a specific purchase.

What is the difference between a secured and unsecured loan?
A secured loan is backed by collateral, such as a vehicle or property, which the lender can claim if you default. Unsecured loans, like most personal loans, do not require collateral but typically carry higher interest rates due to increased risk to the lender.

What credit score do I need to qualify?
Requirements vary by loan type. Personal loans generally require a minimum credit score of 650. Mortgages and auto loans may be available to applicants with scores as low as 600, though a lower score usually results in a higher interest rate.

How long does loan approval take?
Personal loan decisions are often made within minutes for online applications and disbursed within one to two business days. Mortgages require a more thorough review process and can take two to four weeks from application to closing.

Can I pay off a loan early?
Yes, most of our loans allow early repayment without penalty. Some mortgage products carry a prepayment penalty during the first three to five years; check your specific loan agreement for details.
