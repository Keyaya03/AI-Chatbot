Frequently Asked Questions: Interest Rates

How are savings account interest rates determined?
Savings account rates are set based on the central bank's benchmark rate along with internal factors such as account tier and balance. Higher-tier accounts with larger balances typically earn a higher annual percentage yield.

Are interest rates fixed or variable?
It depends on the product. Savings accounts typically have variable rates that can change with market conditions. Fixed-term products like GICs and certain mortgages lock in a rate for the duration of the term, protecting you from rate increases.

How often is interest calculated and paid?
Interest on savings accounts is typically calculated daily and paid out monthly. Loan interest is usually calculated based on the outstanding balance and charged according to the schedule outlined in your loan agreement, often monthly.

What is APR and how does it differ from interest rate?
The interest rate is the cost of borrowing expressed as a percentage of the principal. The Annual Percentage Rate, or APR, includes the interest rate plus additional fees such as origination fees, giving a more complete picture of the total cost of a loan.

Can my interest rate change after I open an account or loan?
Variable-rate products can change if the underlying benchmark rate changes. Fixed-rate products remain the same for the agreed term. Credit card interest rates can also increase if you miss payments, triggering a penalty APR as outlined in your cardholder agreement.
