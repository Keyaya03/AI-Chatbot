Frequently Asked Questions: Account Opening

What types of accounts can I open?
We offer chequing accounts, savings accounts, and youth accounts for customers under 18. Chequing accounts are designed for everyday transactions, while savings accounts offer higher interest rates for customers looking to grow their balance over time.

What documents do I need to open an account?
You will need two pieces of government-issued identification, proof of address such as a utility bill or lease agreement, and your Social Insurance Number for tax reporting purposes. Non-residents may open an account with a valid passport and additional documentation verifying their address abroad.

Is there a minimum deposit required?
Most chequing accounts require no minimum opening deposit. Savings accounts typically require a minimum deposit of $25 to activate interest accrual. Premium accounts with additional benefits may require a minimum balance of $5,000 to avoid monthly fees.

Can I open an account online?
Yes, most personal accounts can be opened entirely online through our website or mobile app. Business accounts and accounts requiring notarized documents must be opened in person at a branch.

How long does it take to open an account?
Online applications are typically approved within one business day. In-branch applications are often completed the same day, with your debit card mailed within five to seven business days.
